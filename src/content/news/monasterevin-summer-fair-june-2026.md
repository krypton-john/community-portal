Join neighbours for the annual Monasterevin Summer Fair at the canal harbour.

## Highlights

- Local food stalls and craft vendors
- Live music from 12:00
- Children's activities and face painting
- Raffle in aid of the community centre fund

Free entry. Dogs welcome on leads. Rain date: Sunday 15 June.
