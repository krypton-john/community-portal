Main Street will be closed to through traffic from **Monday 2 June to Friday 6 June** while resurfacing works take place.

## Diversions

- Traffic heading south: use Station Road → Canal Walk
- Traffic heading north: use Dublin Street → Ballynafagh Road
- Local access maintained for residents and businesses

## Parking

On-street parking suspended on Main Street during works. The car park near the train station remains open.

Pedestrian access to shops will be maintained via marked walkways.
