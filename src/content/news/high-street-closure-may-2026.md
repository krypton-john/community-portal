High Street will be closed to through traffic from **Monday 2 June to Friday 6 June** while resurfacing works take place.

## Diversions

- Northbound traffic: use Mill Lane → Church Road
- Southbound traffic: use Station Approach → Riverside Way
- Local access maintained for residents and businesses with permits

## Parking

On-street parking suspended on High Street during works. The car park on Station Approach remains open.

Pedestrian access to shops will be maintained via marked walkways.
