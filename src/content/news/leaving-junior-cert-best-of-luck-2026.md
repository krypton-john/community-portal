This week, students across Monasterevin and the wider area begin their **Leaving Certificate** and **Junior Certificate** exams. To every student sitting papers — **the very best of luck**. You've worked hard to get here, and the whole community is behind you.

## A note for students and parents

Exam season can feel overwhelming. If you or your family need practical tips on preparation, stress, and what comes after the Leaving Cert, [SpunOut's Leaving Cert preparation guide](https://spunout.ie/education/leaving-cert-cao/leaving-cert-preparation-guide/) is an excellent free resource — written for young people, with clear advice on study, wellbeing, and the CAO process.

Take a breath, do your best, and remember — this is one chapter, not the whole story. Go n-éirí an t-ádh libh!
